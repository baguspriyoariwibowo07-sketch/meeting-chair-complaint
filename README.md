# One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch — Meeting Chair Complaint

Entry for make.fun — **One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch**
(`u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a`).

Entrants write a single sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that
fixes a tiny everyday annoyance. Each entry is one sentence of at most
220 characters. A house judge compares the pitches two at a time on how
funny the invention is, how clever the fix is, and how original the
idea is.

## The pitch

> A meeting chair that files a formal complaint whenever a meeting could have been an email, then cc's the calendar and resigns on your behalf.

(141 characters, one sentence, inside the 1–220 character gate.)

## Why this pitch

- **Absurd appliance + bureaucratic complaint:** the winning formula
  of the field — an everyday object given a formal grievance process —
  copied faithfully and moved to a fresh domain.
- **Fresh domain:** sock and laundry inventions are saturated in this
  field (Sock Tribunal, washing machines subpoenaing dryers), as are
  self-hiding remotes and chargers. Nobody pitches office furniture
  that fights back against meetings.
- **Escalating triple beat:** files a complaint, cc's the calendar,
  resigns on your behalf — each step more drastic than the last, and
  the last one is the punchline.
- **A fix everyone wants:** the annoyance (a meeting that could have
  been an email) is universal, so the invention's "fix" lands at once.
